# ODBC binding for the `odbc` connector -- the escape hatch for backends with no
# SQLAlchemy dialect of their own (legacy ERPs, Progress, Informix, older Sybase).
#
# The WHEEL alone is not enough and the connector says so: pyodbc is a binding,
# not a driver. A host also needs a driver manager (unixODBC) and a registered
# vendor driver, which are per-vendor and often licence-gated, so they stay the
# operator's choice rather than image weight every deployment pays for. That is
# exactly what `_odbc_usable` probes, and why it asks pyodbc.drivers() rather
# than merely importing the module.
